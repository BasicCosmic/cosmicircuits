Mission Overview

Bengaluru's
1-day hardware
mission.

Part of Hack Club Blueprint's global Build Guilds week — running April 13–19 across 100+ cities. Every guild is local. Every build is real.

01 Who Any teen aged 13–18. Zero hardware experience required — bring curiosity and a phone. Beginners and serial makers both welcome.
02 Where A curated, hardware-friendly space in Bengaluru. Venue confirmed closer to the event — sign up to get the details direct.
03 What Workshops, project demos, lightning talks, soldering sessions, open build time. Makers colliding at full speed for one full day.
04 Cost Completely free. Hack Club funds each guild per attendee — food, venue, and materials included. You bring the ideas.
05 Scale 100+ guilds running globally the same week. Same energy, same mission — Bengaluru on the map alongside every other city.
06 Blueprint Hack Club's hardware program, built with AMD. Grants up to $400 USD for advanced builds, real parts, real guidance for teen makers.

Cosmic Circuits is Bengaluru's edition of Build Guild — a 1-day hardware meetup running as part of Hack Club Blueprint's global week (April 13–19). Free, beginner-friendly, and open to any teen who wants to build, demo, and connect.

100% free. Hack Club funds each guild per attendee. No tickets, no fees — you pay nothing. Food is also covered on the day.

Not at all. Build Guilds welcome complete beginners alongside makers who've shipped 10+ projects. We run beginner workshops and open build time for those with their own work.

Any teen aged 13–18. You don't need to be a Hack Club member — if you're in that age range, you're in.

The venue is being finalised. Sign up and you'll receive full venue details as soon as they're confirmed via your attendee dashboard.

A laptop (highly recommended), your phone (required), and any hardware projects you'd like to demo. Tools like soldering irons, multimeters, or breadboards are welcome — we may run some tournaments.

A parental guide will be published closer to the event. You'll receive it automatically once you sign up.

Hack Club is a nonprofit with 50,000+ teen makers worldwide. Blueprint is their hardware program built with AMD — giving teens real parts and grants up to $400 USD. Learn more →
